Data Deletion Instructions

How to ask us to erase the messages and contact details we hold about you, including anything we received through Facebook or Instagram.

Effective 11 August 2026 Version 1.0 Completed within 30 days

The short version

Email outbound@eazy.chat with the subject line “Data deletion request”, and tell us which account you messaged us from — your phone number, your Instagram or Facebook username, or the email address you wrote from.

We delete what we hold and confirm in writing, within 30 days. There is no charge and you do not need an account with us.

ITS - Innovative Tech Solutions, Unipessoal, Lda operates eazy.chat, the platform where messages sent to our WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, email and website chat channels are received and answered. If you have contacted us on any of those, we hold a record of that conversation. This page explains how to have it erased. What we hold and why is described in full in our Privacy Policy.

How to make the request

  1. Write to us

    Send an email to outbound@eazy.chat with “Data deletion request” as the subject.

  2. Tell us how to find you

    We identify conversations by the account they came from, so include whichever of these apply:

    • the phone number you used on WhatsApp;
    • your Instagram username, or the Facebook profile name you used;
    • the email address you wrote to us from;
    • roughly when you contacted us, and which ITS brand or account — helpful if you are not sure.

  3. Confirm it is you

    We may write back asking you to confirm your identity — for example by replying from the same email address, or by sending the request from the same messaging account. This protects you: we are not going to delete or disclose someone's conversation history on the word of a stranger. We ask for the least we need and nothing more.

  4. We delete it and tell you

    We locate every conversation linked to that account, delete it, and email you to confirm what was removed and whether anything had to be retained under section 5.

Facebook and Instagram

If you contacted us through a Facebook Page or an Instagram account belonging to ITS, there are two separate things you may want to do. They are not the same, and doing only the first will not erase anything.

  1. Stop any further data reaching us

    On Facebook, open Settings & privacy › Settings › Apps and Websites, find the ITS application in the list, and choose Remove. On Instagram, you can block or restrict our account. From that point on, Meta sends us nothing further about you.

    This revokes access going forward. It does not delete the messages we already received.

  2. Ask us to erase what we already hold

    Email outbound@eazy.chat as described in section 2, and name the Instagram username or Facebook profile you messaged us from. We delete the conversation, the platform-issued identifier Meta gave us for you, your display name and profile picture, and every message and attachment in that thread.

Deleting your data with us has no effect on the copy Meta holds. Facebook and Instagram are operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited as a separate controller, and to remove your data from Facebook or Instagram themselves you need to use Meta's own settings and tools.

What we delete

On a completed request, we remove from eazy.chat:

  • every message you sent us and every reply in that conversation, on every channel you name;
  • all attachments — images, audio, video and documents — sent in those conversations;
  • your contact record: name, display name, username, phone number, email address, profile picture and any custom attributes stored against you;
  • the platform-issued identifiers linking that contact to Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp;
  • internal notes and labels our agents attached to those conversations.

Deletion is permanent and is not reversible. Copies may persist in encrypted server backups for a short period until those backups rotate out of retention; they are not accessible for ordinary use and are overwritten in the normal cycle.

What we may have to keep

In a small number of cases the law requires us to retain something despite a deletion request. Where that happens we tell you exactly what is being kept and why, and we delete everything else. The usual cases are:

  • Invoices and accounting records — Portuguese tax law requires these to be kept for 10 years.
  • An open dispute or legal claim — records needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim, kept only for as long as that is necessary.
  • A record of your request itself — we keep a note of what you asked for and what we did, for 3 years, so that we can demonstrate we complied. That note contains the minimum needed and does not preserve the deleted conversations.

How long it takes

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them, and usually much sooner. If a request is unusually complex we may extend that by up to two further months under Article 12(3) of the GDPR, and we will tell you within the first month if that happens, and why. There is no charge for making a request.

Your other rights

Deletion is one of several rights you have under the GDPR. You can also ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, have it corrected, restrict how we use it, or object to our using it at all. Those rights and how to exercise them are set out in section 13 of our Privacy Policy.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, please tell us first at outbound@eazy.chat. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134 — 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, Portugal — www.cnpd.pt.

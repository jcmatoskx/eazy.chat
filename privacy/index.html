Who we are

The controller of the personal data described in this policy is ITS - Innovative Tech Solutions, Unipessoal, Lda, a company registered in Portugal under VAT and corporate number PT515363677, with its registered office at Largo Asilo da Gandarinha, n.º 128, 3720-362 Vila de Cucujães, Oliveira de Azeméis, Portugal.

For anything in this policy, including requests to access or delete your data, write to outbound@eazy.chat. We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer and have not appointed one; that address reaches the people responsible for these requests.

What this policy covers

eazy.chat is the customer-support platform that ITS operates for its own customer communications. It is built on Chatwoot and runs at app.eazy.chat. It is internal tooling: there is no public sign-up, and only authorised ITS personnel have accounts.

This policy covers two different things, and they are easy to confuse:

  • This website (eazy.chat) — the pages you are reading now. Covered in section 3.
  • The platform (app.eazy.chat) — where messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, email or website chat are received and answered. Covered in sections 4 to 14.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage, runs no analytics or tracking of any kind, embeds no social widgets, and loads no resources from any other domain — the typeface, icons and styles are all served from eazy.chat itself. There is no form on this site and nothing to submit.

Our hosting provider, GitHub, Inc., processes your IP address and browser user agent in server logs as a technical necessity of delivering the pages, in the same way any web server does. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them.

What we process when you contact us

When you send us a message on any connected channel, that message and the information attached to it by the channel are received into the eazy.chat inbox so that a member of our team can read and answer it. Depending on the channel, that typically includes:

  • your name or the display name and handle on your account, and your profile picture where the channel supplies one;
  • the identifier the channel uses for you — for example a phone number, an email address, or a platform-issued user ID;
  • the content of your messages and any files, images, audio or documents you attach;
  • timestamps, delivery and read status, and which channel the message arrived on;
  • notes, labels and internal comments our agents add to the conversation in order to handle it;
  • anything else you choose to tell us in the message — an order number, an address, a complaint.

Please do not send us special category data. We do not ask for and do not need information about your health, religion, political opinions, trade union membership, sex life or sexual orientation, biometric or genetic data. If you send it anyway we will handle it confidentially and delete it when it is no longer needed for the request you made.

Facebook and Instagram

ITS connects the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that it owns to eazy.chat, using the official Meta APIs, so that direct messages and comments sent to those accounts can be answered from the same inbox as everything else. Meta refers to the information this makes available to us as Platform Data. This section describes exactly what we do with it.

What we receive

  • the page-scoped or Instagram-scoped identifier that Meta issues for you — an identifier specific to your conversation with our account, not your Facebook or Instagram account ID;
  • your display name, username and profile picture as shown by Meta;
  • the content of the direct messages, comments and attachments you send to our accounts, and their timestamps;
  • access tokens issued to our own business accounts, which authorise the connection. These are credentials belonging to ITS, not information about you.

What we do with it

We use it for exactly one purpose: to display your conversation to an ITS support agent and let them reply to you. It is stored in the same conversation record as every other channel so that our team can see the history of what you have asked us.

What we do not do with it

  • We do not sell, rent or licence it to anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising, ad targeting, look-alike audiences or any marketing to you that you did not ask for.
  • We do not use it to build profiles about you, to score you, or to make automated decisions about you.
  • We do not use it to determine eligibility for employment, housing, credit, insurance or any similar decision.
  • We do not transfer it to data brokers, ad networks or information resellers.
  • We do not combine it with data bought from third parties.

Our use of Platform Data is subject to the Meta Platform Terms and the Meta Developer Policies, and we handle it in accordance with them. Meta Platforms Ireland Limited is a separate controller for the processing it carries out on Facebook and Instagram itself; its handling of your data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy, not by this one.

You can disconnect us at any time from Settings › Apps and Websites in your Facebook account, or by blocking our account on Instagram. That stops any further data reaching us. To have what we already hold erased, follow our data deletion instructions.

WhatsApp

Where a WhatsApp Business channel is connected, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the content and timestamps of the messages and attachments you send to that number. It is used to answer you and is subject to the same restrictions listed in section 5. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and its own handling of your data is governed by the WhatsApp privacy policy.

Email and website chat

Email sent to an ITS address connected to the platform is received into the same inbox, including your email address, display name, the message body and any attachments. Our email is delivered by MXroute (see section 9).

Where a website chat widget is in use, we receive the messages you type into it, the page you were on when you started the conversation, your browser user agent and IP address, and any name or email address you choose to give. The widget sets a cookie or uses local storage in your browser for the sole purpose of keeping your conversation attached to you between page loads. This site — the one you are reading — carries no such widget.

Legal bases

Under Article 6(1) of the GDPR we rely on the following bases, depending on why you contacted us:

Performance of a contract, or steps before entering one — Art. 6(1)(b)
Where your message concerns an order, a purchase, a service we provide to you, or a request for a quotation.
Legitimate interests — Art. 6(1)(f)
Our interest in receiving, answering and keeping a record of support enquiries, in running the support operation efficiently, and in protecting the platform against abuse. We consider this proportionate because you initiated the contact, the data is what you chose to send us, and it is used only to answer you. You may object at any time under section 13.
Legal obligation — Art. 6(1)(c)
Where we must keep records to comply with Portuguese tax, accounting or consumer protection law.
Consent — Art. 6(1)(a)
Only where we have asked you for it explicitly and separately. You can withdraw it at any time without affecting what was done before.

Who else can access it

Your data is accessible to the ITS employees and contractors who staff customer support, and to the following processors and providers, each acting under contract and only on our instructions or in their own defined role:

netcup GmbH (Germany)
Infrastructure provider for the server that runs app.eazy.chat. The server is located within the European Union.
MXroute (United States)
Email hosting for our eazy.chat mailboxes. See section 10.
Meta Platforms Ireland Limited
Operator of Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, acting as an independent controller for the processing that happens on those platforms.
GitHub, Inc.
Hosting for this website only. It never receives platform conversation data.

We may also disclose data to our professional advisers, or to a public authority or court where we are legally required to. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Transfers outside the EEA

The conversation data held in the platform is stored on servers inside the European Union.

Two exceptions apply. Our email provider, MXroute, operates the mail server for our eazy.chat addresses in the United States, so email you send to those addresses is stored there. This website is served by GitHub, Inc., a United States company. In both cases the transfer is made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards by writing to outbound@eazy.chat.

Meta may process data on its own account outside the EEA under its own safeguards, which are described in its privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations for as long as we need them to answer you and to handle any follow-up, and then according to these periods:

  • Support conversations: 24 months from the date of the last message in the conversation, after which they are deleted.
  • Conversations connected to an order, invoice or contract: retained for as long as Portuguese tax and accounting law requires, currently 10 years.
  • Data subject requests (access, deletion and similar): a record of the request and our response is kept for 3 years, so that we can demonstrate we complied.

If you ask us to delete your data earlier, we will — see section 14.

How we protect it

The platform is served over encrypted connections (TLS) and hosted on a dedicated server in the EU. Access requires an individual account; accounts are issued only to authorised ITS personnel and are removed when someone leaves or no longer needs access. Connections to Meta are made through official APIs using tokens held by ITS, and those tokens are not shared outside the platform. Backups are taken of the server. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach occurs that is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will notify you and the supervisory authority as the GDPR requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights over the personal data we hold about you:

  • Access — to be told whether we hold data about you and to receive a copy of it (Art. 15).
  • Rectification — to have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed (Art. 16).
  • Erasure — to have your data deleted where we no longer have a valid reason to keep it (Art. 17).
  • Restriction — to have us stop using your data while a dispute about it is resolved (Art. 18).
  • Portability — to receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller (Art. 20).
  • Objection — to object to processing we base on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
  • Withdrawal of consent — where we asked for consent, to take it back at any time (Art. 7(3)).

Write to outbound@eazy.chat to exercise any of these. We will respond within one month. If your request is unusually complex we may extend that by up to two further months, and we will tell you within the first month if we do. There is no charge. We may ask you for information that lets us confirm you are the person the data is about — for example, sending the request from the same email address or naming the account you messaged us from.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to erase the messages and contact details we hold about you at any time. Our data deletion page gives the exact steps, including the specific route for data received through Facebook and Instagram. Requests are completed within 30 days and we confirm in writing when it is done.

We may have to keep some records despite a deletion request — for example invoices we are legally obliged to retain. If that applies to you we will say so, tell you exactly what is being kept and why, and delete everything else.

Children

The platform is not directed at children and we do not knowingly process the data of anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to outbound@eazy.chat and we will delete it.

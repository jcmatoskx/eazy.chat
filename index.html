What the platform does

eazy.chat runs on Chatwoot. These are the parts our support team uses every day.

  • One shared inbox

    Messages from every connected channel land in the same queue. Nothing sits unread in an app nobody opened today.

  • Assignment and teams

    Every conversation has an owner and routes to the team that can actually answer it. Hand-offs carry the full history with them.

  • Canned responses and rules

    Common answers are written once. Automation rules label, route and follow up, so agents spend their time on the messages that need a person.

  • Reporting

    Volume, response times and resolution by channel, team and agent — so the support operation can be measured instead of guessed at.
